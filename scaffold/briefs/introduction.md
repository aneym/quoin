# Introduction

Say who the person is and why the piece is worth reading. Do not summarize the piece. Do not list prestige affiliations. Use the surname when pronouns are unstated.

Word-count band: 80-150 words.

Worked example:
```
Kanekar builds developer platforms for a living, and this piece comes out
of watching the same integration break in three different companies. The
argument is narrow and specific: forward-deployed engineers are not a
staffing decision, they are a signal about where a product still fails.
Read it for the failure, not the org chart.
```

Common rejection reasons:
- Restating the piece's argument instead of the reason to read it (a piece
  summary, not an introduction).
- Listing where the author works, studied, or has spoken (a prestige
  listing; the reader doesn't need a résumé to trust the argument).
- Guessing "he" or "she" from a name when the evidence never states a
  pronoun (use the surname instead).
