# Title deck

Name the book's subject and governing question in concrete language. Do not make claims that the sources do not support.

Word-count band: 15-40 words.

Worked example:
```
What survives when the furniture is stripped away? Three sources, one
question, and the failure classes that decide whether an ingestion
pipeline can be trusted.
```

Common rejection reasons:
- Running long: a title deck that reads like an introduction has missed
  its job. Keep it inside the word-count band.
- Making a claim the sources don't back, because a title deck is the one
  block a skimming reader takes at face value.
