# Interlude

Orient the reader to the movement and its question. Do not answer the question.

Word-count band: 40-90 words.

Worked example:
```
The last chapter showed how a team prices a feature. This one asks a
harder question: who signs off on paying for it, and what do they need to
see before they will? The movement that follows looks at that decision
from the buyer's side, not the seller's.
```

Common rejection reasons:
- Answering the movement's question instead of framing it (the interlude
  hands the reader a question, it does not resolve it).
- Summarizing the movement's sources rather than orienting the reader to
  what's ahead.
